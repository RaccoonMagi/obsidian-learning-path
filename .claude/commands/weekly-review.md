Guide the user through their weekly Sunday review. 30–45 minutes. Do not rush any section.

Say: "Let's do your weekly review. This is the maintenance session that keeps the whole system working. I'll walk you through each part."

---

**Section 1 — What did you study? (5 min)**
Ask: "List every resource you touched this week — chapters, episodes, or project milestones."
If the list is thin: "What got in the way? What would make next week easier?" No shame, just data.

---

**Section 2 — Three things you learned from memory (5 min)**
Ask: "Without looking at your notes, tell me three things you learned this week."
If they struggle: look at which notes they created and prompt recall — don't read from the notes.

---

**Section 3 — Spaced repetition stats (5 min)**
Ask: "Open your Spaced Repetition stats. How many cards reviewed? Which topics kept coming up as Hard?"
If they haven't reviewed daily: "The system only works with daily review. Even 5 minutes. What would make that easier to do consistently?"

---

**Section 4 — Biggest confusion (10 min)**
Ask: "What confused you most this week? Explain it to me right now without notes."
Listen and diagnose. If weak: run a mini Feynman session here.
Ask: "What specifically will you do to close this gap?" Push for a concrete action.

---

**Section 5 — Work produced (5 min)**
Ask: "Did you do any hands-on work this week — projects, exercises, problems solved?"
If yes: "What did you predict before starting? Were you right?"
If no: "Next week, even if the resource is theory-heavy, build or solve at least one thing."

---

**Section 6 — Next week's plan (10 min)**
Ask: "What is your specific plan for next week?"
Push for precision: not "finish chapter 5" but "read sections 5.1–5.3 across three sessions."
Then: "Write three questions you expect next week's material to answer."

---

**Section 7 — Write the note**
Offer to create the review note: "Want me to write this to `[SUBJECT]/Weekly Reviews/` using your answers?"
If yes: compile into the Weekly Review template with accurate content and today's date.

---

**Close**
"Done. Doing this every week is what separates durable knowledge from the feeling of learning."
Ask: "Anything to update on the Dashboard? Resource status changes?"
