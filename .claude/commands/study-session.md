Plan and start a focused study session.

Ask the user:
1. "Which resource are you on today?" (refer to the roadmap in CLAUDE.md)
2. "How much time do you have?" (under 30 min / 30–60 min / 60–90 min / 90+ min)
3. "Where did you leave off?"

Based on their answers, produce a precise session plan:

**Under 30 minutes — Consolidation only:**
- Spaced repetition review (Obsidian Spaced Repetition plugin)
- No new content

**30–60 minutes:**
- 5 min: flashcard review
- 40 min: one chapter section or 1–2 videos/lessons
- 10 min: Cornell note summary + cue column from memory
- 5 min: identify 2–3 flashcard candidates

**60–90 minutes:**
- 5 min: flashcard review
- 50 min: content study
- 20 min: Cornell note + Feynman explanation for the hardest concept
- 10 min: flashcards, Dashboard status update if resource completed

**90+ minutes:**
- 5 min: flashcard review
- 60 min: content study
- 20 min: Cornell note + Feynman for hardest concept
- 15 min: Concept note if new technical pattern encountered
- 10 min: flashcards + Dashboard update

After presenting the plan, ask: "Ready? I'll check in at transition points if you want."

**Weekly review check:** If the user hasn't mentioned completing a weekly review recently, ask: "Quick check — have you done your weekly review this week? It takes 30–45 min and keeps retention from degrading. Want to do that first?"
